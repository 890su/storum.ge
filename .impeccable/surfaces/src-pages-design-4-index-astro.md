## Direction contract

**Thesis.** A warehouse receiving ticket that calculates storage before asking a business for their contact details.

**Own-world.** Perforated ivory paper on dock navy, narrow printed rules, blue-black ink, and safety orange reserved for one pallet place and the next action.

**Story.** Know the unit rate and what is included; adjust a shipment; request a confirmed quote. Private customers have a secondary route.

**First viewport.** Title and unit rate above a single sheet with a pallet diagram, labeled volume and duration fields, optional handling and a live total. On mobile the amount and calculator move ahead of the decorative diagram.

**Form.** Warehouse receiving ticket, approved in chat as a direction rather than an exact reproduction of the illustrated prices. seed: none (unavailable).

**Finish.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Direction contract

**Thesis.** An architectural warehouse section that explains the pallet place as the paid unit, then lets businesses price their shipment.

**Own-world.** Ivory drawing stock, graphite structure, slanted lettering, dimension lines and ochre for one pallet and the quote action.

**Story.** Know the unit rate and what is included; adjust a shipment; request a confirmed quote. Private customers have a secondary route.

**First viewport.** A narrow title-and-rate column left of a dominant edge-reaching warehouse cutaway; a shallow four-part calculator overlaps the bottom of the section. On mobile the drawing is stacked above a compact two-column calculator.

**Form.** Warehouse Cross-Section, fourth concept selected in chat. Visual reference: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/challenger-section.webp`. The drawing is illustrative; calculations and specifications use verified tariffs. seed: none (unavailable).

**Finish.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
